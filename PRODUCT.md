Distinctive, production-grade interface design eliminating generic AI slop through curated typography, refined color palettes, fluid animations, and strict design engineering rules.

Modern web applications, interactive dashboards, mobile-responsive tools, and presentation surfaces.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Verified Top 10 UI/UX, Frontend, and Apple Design skills actively loaded
- Accessible semantic HTML primitives with full keyboard and screen reader support
- Fluid spring physics and compositor-only animations without layout thrashing
- Apple HIG spatial consistency and material design depth

## Brand Commitments
- High typographic contrast and calibrated color scales
- Elimination of generic placeholders, saturated AI gradients, and arbitrary spacing
- Intentional layout hierarchy and purposeful motion

## Product Principles
1. **Design Distinction**: Deliver award-winning craft rather than generic default templates.
2. **Fluid Physical Motion**: Use natural springs, interruptible gestures, and frame-budget-conscious animations.
3. **Rigorous Quality Floor**: Validate accessibility, responsive scaling, and interaction feedback on every surface.
